# Tech Sector Q3 Analysis

## Executive Summary
This report summarises **illustrative sample data** for eight fictional technology companies. It is used by the `--demo` mode to exercise the PDF compiler without calling any LLM or search API.

## Market Overview
Large platform vendors continued to dominate revenue, while smaller AI-focused names posted the fastest growth. Margins remain widest in semiconductors and cloud infrastructure.

## Key Findings
- Revenue is concentrated in the top three companies.
- Growth is inversely related to company size in this sample.
- R&D intensity is highest among the fastest growing firms.

## Financial Analysis
The summary statistics table at the end of this report shows the mean, minimum, maximum and standard deviation of each metric. A wide standard deviation on growth indicates an uneven market.
